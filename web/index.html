AquaSentinel — a monitoring prototype. Every number on this dashboard is badged measured, derived, projected or forecast, so an estimate can never pass for a reading.

Thresholds are site configuration, not public-safety limits. Use official meteorological alerts for any public heat or flood warning. Detection figures come from eval/run_eval.py and are shown with their sample counts under Devices & history.
